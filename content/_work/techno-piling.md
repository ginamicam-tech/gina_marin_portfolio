    ## About the project

    Techno Piling is a specialist geotechnical engineering company whose communication needed a recognisable presence for the North American market. The project concerned the relationship between technical expertise and public identity: how could a specialist business communicate clearly and consistently across its materials? The existing brief identifies stability, precision and structural force as starting points for the visual language. The archive includes a logo family, geometric research, communication layouts, vehicle applications and brand guidelines. Together, these pieces show the range of practical settings in which the identity needed to work, from an individual mark to recurring digital communication and company materials.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## My part

    I developed the brand identity, applications and guidelines for an international audience, translating structural references into a compact mark and geometric visual language. Alongside the identity, I planned and created regular social content, keeping the communication consistent across formats.
