## Context & Challenge

The Municipality of Alta Gracia communicates with people through public information, tourism, cultural activity and civic events. Between 2019 and 2023, my work within the Department of Technology, Innovation & Design supported this communication through audiovisual production. The material covered different subjects and viewing situations: a public works programme, a tourism invitation, an institutional opening, seasonal activity, a cultural testimony and employment information. Each required a clear editorial focus while remaining part of municipal communication. The selected videos below preserve those differences in subject and format, showing how public stories were prepared for mobile viewing as well as wider screens.

## Approach & Contribution

As Audiovisual Producer and Creative Project Lead, I supported the communications team by shaping supplied footage into public-facing stories. My contribution connected editorial decisions with practical delivery: selecting a sequence, establishing a rhythm and adapting the format to the intended channel. The cultural testimony gives priority to voice and lived experience, while the public works and tourism pieces organise places and activity into a legible account. The portfolio retains both social and landscape edits so the actual outputs can be viewed in context. These videos demonstrate audiovisual communication and team support; they do not imply that I filmed every source image or independently directed the municipality’s wider strategy.
