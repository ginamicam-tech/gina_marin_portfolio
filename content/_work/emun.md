## Context & Challenge

Bring order and professional presence to a young electrical and lighting company serving wholesale and retail customers.

## Approach & Contribution

The visual idea connects energy, light and the letter E. A bold modular symbol gives the brand consistency without making it rigid. Concept and logo development, colour and type, visual modules, catalogue direction, social media templates, uniforms and vehicle graphics.
