    ## About the project

    Techno Piling is a specialist geotechnical engineering company. This project involved redesigning its existing brand identity to support its entry into the U.S. market. The project concerned the relationship between technical expertise and public identity: how could a specialist business communicate clearly and consistently across its materials? The existing brief identifies stability, precision and structural force as starting points for the visual language. The archive includes a logo family, geometric research, communication layouts, vehicle applications and brand guidelines. Together, these pieces show the range of practical settings in which the identity needed to work, from an individual mark to recurring digital communication and company materials.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## My part

    I redesigned the brand identity and developed applications and guidelines to support the company’s entry into the U.S. market, translating structural references into a compact mark and geometric visual language. Alongside the identity, I planned and created regular social content, keeping the communication consistent across formats.
