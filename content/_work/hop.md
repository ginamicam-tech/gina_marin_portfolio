    ## About the project

    The Poetics of the Collective brought people together at House of Play in Copenhagen from noon on 14 March to noon on 15 March 2026. It concluded the first six-month Playlab cycle, exploring how individual contributions can become a shared artistic experience.

    Music, movement, meals, rest and everyday tasks became material for an unfolding performance. Participants moved between observing and contributing, allowing small gestures to develop into collective scenes. The experiment took place in a sex-inclusive setting, with consent, personal boundaries and responsibility for one another shaping the work.
