## Context & Challenge

Cuerpo.Interfaz is the research foundation behind my practice in visual arts and digital media. It investigates the body as an interface between physical and digital space through performance, material processes, photography and transmedia experimentation. The question is approached through work rather than treated as a problem with one final answer. A wearable membrane, a photographic sequence and an everyday image archive each make different relationships visible. The research brings body, material, space and image into contact, allowing an experiment in one medium to inform another. Its documentation provides a way to follow those connections and understand how artistic enquiry develops through making.

## Approach & Contribution

I developed material and image-based experiments as part of this research practice. In Clinical Fold, white plastic was sewn into a wearable membrane and explored through pressure, gesture and photoperformance. A photographic sequence made bodily presence and fragmentation visible. In Self Archive, daily records of migration and life in Copenhagen were combined with portraits, architectural details and double exposure. Repetition and montage became ways to examine how an image of the self changes across places and interfaces. The resulting documentation and modular archive inform ongoing questions about presence, interaction and learning. The research page remains available below with the original process descriptions and visual sequences.
