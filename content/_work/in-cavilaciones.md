    ## About the work

    *Cavilaciones atravesadas* is an experimental publishing project developed in the Graphic Editing seminar at the National University of Córdoba. It gives form to fragmented thoughts and the overload of information within the creative process. Conceived as a space for graphic and narrative experimentation, the book creates a physical rhythm through texture, folds and changing layers rather than a linear story.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Making the work

    I first constructed a handmade paper prototype, combining sandpaper, papers of different weights, transparent sheets, and black and red inks. These materials became part of the visual language: figures emerge through layered surfaces, while folds and textures invite a tactile reading.

    I then scanned and digitally edited the prototype, translating its material qualities into an editorial sequence. This process led to two printed editions of the illustrated book, both published under the same title, *Cavilaciones atravesadas*. The project brings together material experimentation, illustration, digital editing and print, tracing the transformation of a unique handmade object into a reproducible publication.
