## Context & Challenge

Techno Piling is a specialist geotechnical engineering company whose communication needed a recognisable presence for the North American market. The project concerned the relationship between technical expertise and public identity: how could a specialist business communicate clearly and consistently across its materials? The existing brief identifies stability, precision and structural force as starting points for the visual language. The archive includes a logo family, geometric research, communication layouts, vehicle applications and brand guidelines. Together, these pieces show the range of practical settings in which the identity needed to work, from an individual mark to recurring digital communication and company materials.

## Approach & Contribution

I reconceptualised the brand for an international audience and developed the identity, application system and brand catalogue. The design work translated structural references into a compact mark and a geometric language that could be reused across communication. Alongside the guidelines, I planned and created regular social content, with the existing record describing a rhythm of two to three posts per week. The practical task was to keep those outputs consistent without treating every piece as a separate design problem. The documented result is a repositioned visual identity, guidelines and applications supporting regular communication. No market growth or commercial performance is inferred from the design work.
