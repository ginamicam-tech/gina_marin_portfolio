    ## Background & Practice

    My artistic background informs the conceptual and methodological foundations of my professional practice, in which research, critical reflection, material experimentation and embodied experience operate as interconnected forms of inquiry. My master's thesis investigates bodily presence, perception and personal agency within contemporary digital environments through performance, drawing, printmaking and photographic archives, reflecting a broader interest in the relationships between the body, technological mediation and the construction of individual and collective experience. These concerns extend into my work with interactive installations, audiovisual practices, exhibition development, cultural storytelling and participatory formats, where I explore how artistic languages can generate alternative forms of perception, interaction and engagement. Alongside my artistic practice, my experience in movement facilitation, educational environments and accessible learning has strengthened my attention to human-centred approaches, the dynamics of participation and the ways people encounter, interpret and contribute to creative processes.

    ## How I Work

    I approach projects through a combination of artistic thinking, conceptual research, collaborative development and practical production, moving between the formulation of ideas, contextual investigation, visual experimentation, technical implementation and public presentation. My experience includes coordinating artistic and cultural initiatives, supporting exhibition production, developing curatorial and communication content, facilitating workshops and working with multidisciplinary teams to translate complex concepts into tangible experiences, visual narratives and accessible forms of engagement. I integrate methods from embodied and human-centred design, participatory research and co-creation with digital tools and creative technologies, including interactive programming with p5.js, audiovisual experimentation and AI-assisted workflows for research, ideation, communication and production. Documentation, dialogue and iterative experimentation are central to my process, allowing projects to evolve through material conditions, collective contributions and the specific contexts in which they take place. Across these different areas of practice, I am particularly interested in connecting artistic vision with organisational structures, communication strategies and collaborative relationships that support the development, continuity and public relevance of cultural projects.

I’m Gina Marin, an interdisciplinary artist and creative practitioner originally from Argentina and currently based in Copenhagen, Denmark. I hold a master's degree in Visual Arts and Digital Media, and my professional experience spans over seven years across contemporary artistic production, cultural project development, curatorial practice, visual communication and participatory initiatives. My work brings together artistic research, conceptual development, storytelling, hands-on experimentation and project coordination, drawing on an interdisciplinary understanding of how ideas are conceived, developed, materialised and communicated within different cultural and social contexts. I collaborate with artists, cultural organisations, educational institutions and communities, contributing to projects that involve creative production, collaborative processes, public engagement and the development of spaces for artistic experimentation and knowledge exchange.
