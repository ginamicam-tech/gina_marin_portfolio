*Chromatic Body* brings together paintings and ink works that explore the body as a space of sensation, memory, and continuous transformation. Developed alongside my practice in performance, movement, and artistic research, the series approaches image-making as an embodied process: a negotiation between gesture, material, and perception. Colour becomes a way of articulating experiences that resist a stable outline, while the relationship between figure and surrounding space opens questions about where the body ends and its environment begins.

Within my broader research into bodily presence and personal agency, these works consider how lived experience takes form before it becomes a fixed or recognisable image. The encounter between oil painting and ink connects the density of flesh with the movement of breath, allowing different rhythms of embodiment to coexist. This inquiry also resonates with my thesis on subjectivity and digital mediation: how can the complexity of a sensing body remain present within the images through which we represent ourselves? *Chromatic Body* develops this question through material practice, allowing ambiguity, vulnerability, and change to remain visible.
