    ## About the project

    Rituals of Collective Descent was presented at Xplore Festival in Berlin in 2026. Within the installation, an interactive monster greeted visitors. Its translucent body and illuminated circular face sat among projections that extended across the creature and the surrounding space.

    ## My part

    I contributed as co-creator, performer and poetry reader, and made the interactive monster that welcomed visitors into the installation. It brought a character into the space for people to encounter as they arrived.
