    ## About the project

    CARE LAB is a participatory environment exploring care, healthcare and future technology, developed for GRASP Festival in connection with Roskilde University. Visitors were invited to pause, notice what needed care, put observations into words and make prints that became part of a shared visual record.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## My part

    I co-developed the installation and its participation formats, working with physical prototypes, printed prompts, materials and shared worktables. The stations connected listening and reflection with labelling and printmaking, giving visitors different ways to respond to the same question about care.
