    ## About the work

    Made in the Graphic Editing seminar, this artist’s book gives form to fragmented thoughts and the overload of information in a creative process. Its pages create a physical rhythm through texture, folds and changing layers rather than a linear story.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Making the work

    I combined sandpaper, papers of different weights, transparent sheets, and black and red inks. Figures and fragments appear within the textures of the pages. I later scanned and digitally edited the book, allowing the physical object and its screen-based version to develop different visual qualities.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Part of the research

    This project is documented as an earlier stage of the enquiry developed in [Body.Interface](/work/body-interface/).
