    ## About the project

    Blindmotion creates opportunities for blind and visually impaired participants to take part in physical activities in Denmark. Accessible teaching and clear programme information help participants, facilitators and partner organisations take part.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## My part

    I co-organise inclusive physical activities and help prepare accessible learning materials for facilitators. The materials draw on universal design principles and support practical decisions about how people can participate.

    I also develop visual and written communication for participants and partners, including event information and the Blindmotion på Højskole postcards. This connects programme coordination with clear invitations and information about taking part.
