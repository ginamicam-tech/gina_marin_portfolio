    ## About the work

    *Pliegue Clínico* brings the body into a confined bathroom of mirrors and water. A white plastic costume covers the head, face and torso, leaving the arms and feet exposed. Against the clean, cold appearance of the setting, breath, slow movement and the folds of the material make bodily presence visible.

    The work explores the tension between being protected and being contained, and between appearing as an image and feeling present in one’s own body.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## My part

    I sewed the plastic on my sewing machine, treating the cuts and seams as drawn lines. Working with butoh movement, I used slowness, twisting and small gestures to test the relationship between the costume and my body. Florencia Aravena photographed the performance.

    I then edited the photographs with heightened brightness, cold colour and white margins, extending the performance into an image sequence. The process also led to *Manifiesto Pulso Butoh*, a poetic text about returning to bodily attention and agency.
