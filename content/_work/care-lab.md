    ## About the project

    Care Lab is a participatory exhibition and workshop presented at GRASP Festival in 2026, developed within the RECRUIT&RETAIN project in collaboration with Roskilde University and partner institutions. It examines care as a relational and professional practice, bringing artistic experimentation into dialogue with healthcare and emerging technologies.

    The exhibition uses installations, reflective questions and collective poster printing to invite discussion of human contact, responsibility and the conditions in which care is provided. Participants translate observations and experiences into words and printed images, contributing to a shared visual record. This process creates a setting for considering how professional knowledge, practical skills and interpersonal attention shape care in medical contexts.

    ## My part

    I contributed to the conceptual development of Care Lab and helped translate its central questions into material and participatory formats. I developed question cards inviting reflection on care within professional medical practice, connecting the exhibition’s broader themes with visitors’ own experiences and interpretations.

    I also conceived and made [Human Warmth](/work/human-warmth/), the installation presented within Care Lab. Alongside this artistic contribution, I co-facilitated the workshop and exhibition, introducing visitors to the project, explaining the installation and supporting participation in reflection and printmaking. My contribution connected conceptual inquiry, installation-making and public engagement.
