I support cultural and creative projects through production, artistic experimentation and visual communication.

Each project starts with its context. Together, we define what needs to happen, choose an approach and develop the work through collaboration, testing and implementation.

## Organisational Development & Creative Production
{: #creative-production}

I support creative and cultural projects by connecting people, resources and ideas through planning, communication and collaborative production.

Discuss the people, purpose, resources and practical constraints that shape the project. Agree on priorities, responsibilities and a workable sequence of tasks. Connect collaborators, organise communication and follow the work through production and implementation. Review what happened, document learning and identify useful next steps.

## Artistic Productions & Dissemination
{: #artistic-production}

My artistic practice explores relationships between bodies, technologies and collective experiences through research, material experimentation and participatory production. Installations, participatory environments and material experiments developed through artistic research, making and public presentation.

Start with a question about bodies, materials, technologies or collective experience. Gather references and explore the context through artistic research. Test materials, physical interactions and spatial arrangements through prototypes. Develop the installation or participatory format, bringing physical and visual elements together. Observe how people encounter the work and use documentation and discussion to inform further experiments.

Questions and experiments inform each other: this is a flexible research process, rather than a fixed sequence.

## Communication Projects
{: #communication}

I develop visual communication systems that connect strategic direction, design and practical applications across digital and physical environments. Visual identities, editorial systems and digital communication that give ideas a clear and consistent public form.

Review the context, audience and existing communication materials. Clarify the message, priorities and intended use of the communication. Develop the visual language, layout and content structure as a coherent system. Apply the system across the digital and physical formats the project needs. Review readability, consistency and practical use, then refine the materials.
