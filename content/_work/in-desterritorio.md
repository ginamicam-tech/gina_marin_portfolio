    ## About the work

    Developed in the Visual Narratives Laboratory seminar, this project asks how a body might inhabit a virtual environment. Organic networks, graphic rhythms and fragments of faces suggest a space made from movement and connection rather than fixed boundaries.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Making the work

    I worked mainly with black ink on white paper, sometimes adding red. Intuitive, repeated marks build a dense visual field in which a face or figure can appear, fragment or merge with its surroundings. Digital editing extends the drawings into further layers and compositions.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## In the exhibition

    I contributed to the organisation and production of *Oscillating Dialectics* at Avellaneda Café in Córdoba in 2019, where I also exhibited IN_Deterritory. The project connected my drawing practice with the shared work of preparing and presenting an exhibition. The programme documents its curatorial text, participating artists and production credits.
