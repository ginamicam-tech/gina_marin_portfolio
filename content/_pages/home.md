The space between an idea and its realisation is not empty. It is where meaning is negotiated, experience takes shape, and collective action becomes possible. What begins as an intuition or speculative proposition acquires significance through its encounter with the material, social and institutional conditions that shape its emergence. Within this process, thinking becomes inseparable from making, as abstract concepts are translated into situated actions and embodied experiences. An initiative extends beyond a predetermined plan; an experience emerges through the interaction of bodies, spaces, technologies and perception; and an exchange transforms individual expression into collective interpretation and shared knowledge. My practice unfolds within this shifting territory between artistic research, creative production, project development and cultural communication, creating conditions through which ideas generate new relationships, challenge established perspectives and open possibilities for collective transformation.
