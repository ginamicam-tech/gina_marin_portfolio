    ## About the installation

    Human Warmth is an installation I made for Care Lab at GRASP Festival in 2026. A large transparent infusion bag hangs from a stand, with red light inside and a label reading “Human Warmth — Solution for Infusion”.

    The work borrows the visual authority of clinical equipment to consider what sustains us beyond a medical procedure. Its measured volume, dosage instructions and warnings describe something that cannot be contained in a bag: the attention and connection we offer one another.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## A prescription for presence

    The label is part of the artwork. Under familiar headings such as composition, dosage and compatibility, technical language gives way to invitations. The solution contains “traces of hope, joy and collective strength”. Its dosage is “As needed. Give generously. Repeat as necessary.” Compatibility depends on “Open minds”, “Kind environments”, “Diverse bodies” and “Collaborative practices”.

    These instructions introduce a different rhythm into the clinical format: “One moment at a time.” The precaution “Do not substitute presence with efficiency” asks what gets lost when care is reduced to a task to complete. The closing invitation brings that question back to an everyday gesture: “Keep warm. Keep close. Share freely.”
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Making the installation

    Before Human Warmth took the form of an infusion bag, I explored an oversized blister pack. These earlier prototypes were a way of developing the concept and searching for a concrete form: using the language of medical packaging to think about presence and care.

    From that exploration, I developed and made the infusion-bag installation, working with a transparent enclosure, red material and light. The suspended form, outlet and printed label bring the familiar appearance of clinical equipment into a sculptural work. The process photographs below trace the earlier blister experiments, material tests, construction components and preparation of the Human Warmth label.
