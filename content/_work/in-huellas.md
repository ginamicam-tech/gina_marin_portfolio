    ## About the work

    The project begins with the feet as a point of contact between the body and the ground. I recorded movements in sequence, moving between bare feet and feet carrying bricks tied with rope. The contrast between lightness and weight became a way to ask what physical effort means when digital movement can feel almost without resistance.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Making the work

    I translated the photographic sequences into drawings, then used transparency, layering and digital composition to make further versions. Repetition gives the images a rhythm, connecting the physical act of stepping with the trace it leaves on paper and on screen.
- layout: newspaper
