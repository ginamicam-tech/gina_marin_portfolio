## Context & Challenge

BLINDMOTION develops opportunities for inclusive participation in Denmark. Its programme communication needs to connect an invitation with practical information that people can understand and use. Participants, ambassadors and partner organisations approach the project from different positions, so a single message has to work across several relationships. The question is how to make an opportunity easier to discover without losing the information needed to join it. The existing documentation includes event communication and a two-sided postcard for Blindmotion på Højskole. Together, these materials show the connection between a public invitation, programme information and communication with the partners who help opportunities reach participants.

## Approach & Contribution

My role combines inclusive communication and project development. I translate project ideas into visual and written formats for participants, ambassadors and partners, while supporting outreach, programme development and coordination with collaborators. This involves considering both the message and its practical use: what someone needs to recognise first, what information follows and how the material supports a conversation. The event communication and postcard provide examples of that work in different formats. They are evidence of communication developed around participation and collaboration, rather than a claim about measured audience growth. My contribution sits within the wider programme and depends on the relationships between its participants and organisers.
