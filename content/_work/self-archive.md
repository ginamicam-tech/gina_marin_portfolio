    ## About the work

    *Self-Archivo* began as a daily record of moving to Denmark and learning to call a new country home. I photographed my face during journeys, workdays and pauses, using the phone that also organised much of my daily life. The repeated gesture records changes in attention, energy and belonging that can be easy to overlook.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Making the archive

    I layered the portraits with offices, corridors, windows and other spaces encountered in Copenhagen. The vertical format of the phone became a horizontal composition, closer to a computer screen. Repeated faces, translucent layers and white lines create a rhythm from still photographs.

    The work values consistency over a polished self-image. Each photograph holds a particular moment; together they show identity being shaped through language, work, movement and place. The accompanying *Manifiesto Cotidiano* extends that enquiry into writing.
