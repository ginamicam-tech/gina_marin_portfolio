    ## About the collective

    Atmosphere was an artist-led collective in Córdoba. We brought individual practices together through exhibitions, performances and live painting, sharing the practical work of preparing spaces, coordinating with others and communicating events.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## My part

    I contributed as an artist and collaborator, connecting making with the preparation of collective projects. This included installation work for [They Do Not Save Life, Nor Sustain the Soul](/portfolio/no-salvan-la-vida/) and performance at [Modus Androginus](/work/modus-androginus/).
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Painting together

    In co-creation with the production company Umbrality, we developed collective painting interventions, sharing materials and making decisions as the work evolved. The photographs document this collaborative process.

    Other events included Labluhaus and Akana Festival, bringing painting, performance and projected imagery into shared live settings.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Projects within the collective

    Modus Androginus; They Do Not Save Life, Nor Sustain the Soul; Altar: Women and LGBTI+ in the 21st Century; and Yellow Poison Land are part of Atmosphere Collective. Each has its own project page, with photographs, context and credits, and remains individually accessible in the Work archive. Explore them below.
