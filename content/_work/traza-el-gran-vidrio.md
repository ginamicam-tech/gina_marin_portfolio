## Context & Challenge

Traza brings together practices that translate and transform signs into perceptual routes. Works by Guillermo Kuitca, Leticia Obeid, León Ferrari and Mariano Sardón move between mapping, writing, touch and interactive media, inviting close reading across the concrete and the ephemeral. The 2020 proposal was developed for Fundación El Gran Vidrio in Córdoba as a complete exhibition plan. Curatorial research, artwork inventory, spatial zoning, elevations and visitor circulation were resolved together. This brought very different scales and media into one coherent sequence, from monumental mixed-media works and handwritten correspondence to Braille interventions and interactive projections.

## Approach & Contribution

Lead development of the exhibition proposal, from curatorial research to the artwork register and project plan. Define the art direction, spatial sequence and visitor experience across 4 artistic practices. Develop the curatorial brochure and visual communication of the proposal. Coordinate technical planning for mounting, supports, electrical access and projections.

## Evidence & Results

Process and spatial logic

## From proposition to public experience.
{: #exhibition-process-title}

01 

### Curatorial frame

A shared proposition connected four practices through trace, language and perception while preserving the specificity of each artist’s work.

02 

### Artwork mapping

A register of 38 works aligned dimensions, media, technical dependencies and mounting requirements before decisions moved into the room.

03 

### Spatial sequence

Colour-coded zoning, sightlines, thresholds and a proposed visitor route organised monumental works, intimate manuscripts and interactive installations into a legible progression.

04 

### Technical resolution

Wall elevations, supports, tablets, electrical access, projection conditions and installation clearances translated the curatorial idea into an actionable exhibition plan.
