## Context & Challenge

CARE LAB is a participatory installation developed for GRASP Festival in connection with Roskilde University. It brings artistic, healthcare, research and festival contexts into a physical setting where visitors can reflect on care. The challenge is to make an abstract and personal subject available through action. Rather than relying only on an explanatory text, the installation offers ways to observe, label and print. Materials, visual communication and spatial arrangement become part of how a person encounters the question. The photographs document suspended printed elements, a worktable and a light-based physical installation, showing the relationship between the material environment and the people moving through it.

## Approach & Contribution

As Installation Designer and Facilitator, I co-created the installation through concept development, prototyping and the testing of physical components and participation flows. I developed interaction formats that combine visual art, materials and audience participation, including stations for observing, labelling and printing. Working across the different contexts involved in the project required attention to both the physical system and the clarity of the invitation to use it. The installation documentation shows how printed communication and material experiments became a shared spatial experience. Its documented outcome is a public setting in which reflection on care could leave physical and visual traces through participants’ actions.
