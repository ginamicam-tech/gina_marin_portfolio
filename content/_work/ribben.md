    ## About the proposal

    **Submitted to the 2026 open call for a public artwork marking International Women’s Day at Nørrebros Runddel, Copenhagen. Selection pending; the work has not been realised.**

    Developed together with Gina Mobilia, *Ribben: The Resonance Circle* proposes a permanent public sculpture measuring eight metres in diameter and three metres in height. Eight corten-steel ribs, arranged in four mirrored pairs, would form an open space that people could enter and pass through. The steel would develop a durable rust patina over time.

    Taking the ribcage as its starting point, the proposal translates protection and room to breathe into a structure that supports without enclosing. Its centre would remain available for everyday encounters, conversations, readings, performances and collective action.

    ## Care as a shared responsibility

    The proposed sculpture faces Jagtvej 69, connecting the site to the 1910 decision to establish an international women’s day. Rather than treating this history as complete, the work asks who is protected, whose voice is heard and who carries the work of care today.

    The ribs would remain still, while optional lighting could suggest expansion and contraction. Resonance here comes from proximity: being supported by others without losing autonomy. The open centre leaves room for political futures that have not yet been defined. Care is proposed as a shared responsibility that turns solidarity into action, rather than a duty placed on women to endure.

    ## A collaborative proposal

    Gina Mobilia and I bring together practices in visual and spatial art, material experimentation, technology, performance and artistic production. The proposal combines a sculptural form with the possibility of gathering, listening, expression and disagreement in public space.

    If selected, the work would be developed with qualified structural and fabrication specialists, preserving its openness for everyday use and future collective expression.
