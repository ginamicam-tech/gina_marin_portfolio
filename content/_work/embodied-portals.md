    ## Entering through the body

    An ongoing Art & Research exploration of live computer vision and embodied interfaces. Using p5.js, I experiment with digital portals that respond to a face, a gesture or a change of position. Movement becomes a way to enter, play with and reshape a digital environment.

    The enquiry continues [Body.Interface](/work/body-interface/): how can we engage with technology while remaining attentive to bodily experience? Here, the screen becomes a place to test the relationship between what a machine detects and what a person chooses to express.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Vibe coding as a sketching process

    I use AI-assisted coding, often called vibe coding, to move between an idea in words and a working sketch. I describe an interaction, try it with my body and revise what happens on screen. Prompts, code and physical testing become part of the same process.

    These prototypes are experiments rather than finished installations. Play, unexpected responses and the limits of detection help me ask what an interface invites us to do—and what it leaves open.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Experiments

    **Body.Interface / IN-Pressure** places questions and choices over a live camera image. “What arrives first?” asks the participant to consider the body, the story and the rule. Its starting point is simple: “Machine detects. Body decides.”

    **Body Candle Tetris** explores a familiar game through bodily movement. Sideways motion, raised hands and crouching are proposed as controls, while blocks accumulate and melt into candles. The sketch brings gesture, balance and transformation into play.

    **Face Galaxy / Dream Reality Machine** explores a poetic world shaped through facial movement and gestures. Geometric spaces, floating words and fragments of text shift around a live camera portrait. The face becomes an interface for navigating questions of visibility, desire and personal boundaries.
    **Cat / Machine** is a short p5.js animation staging an encounter between a cat and its robotic counterpart. Humour and dialogue explore the gap between calculation and understanding.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## An expanding enquiry

    These sketches sit alongside my [Body Interface archive on Are.na](https://www.are.na/body_interface-archive-across-body-space-systems), where images, texts and references gather new connections. The archive supports the questions; the prototypes let me try them through movement and response.

    Together, they explore how technology might make room for curiosity, bodily agency and poetic encounters.
