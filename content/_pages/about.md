## Developing projects through making and learning.
{: #about-story-title}

I develop, build and implement projects across making, technology and learning environments. My work includes participatory installations, physical prototypes, visual communication and collaborative production.

A master’s degree in Visual Arts and Digital Media supports a practice grounded in material experimentation and research. Experience in exhibition production, community coordination and embodied learning informs how I work with people, spaces and tools.

## Artworks / Independent practice
{: #artistic-series}

Working premise The body records what language cannot hold. Gesture becomes trace. Repetition becomes pulse. Colour becomes a field of relation.

Drawing, colour and repetition explore the body as a sensitive interface between matter, memory and perception.

## Body. *Interface.*
Ink fragments the figure into signals, scars and visual pressure. The body appears as a sensitive territory between matter, memory and mediation.

- Fragment
- Trace
- Memory
- Mediation

Practice in motion

## A body can be **an archive.****a surface.****a signal.****a pulse.**
Across the work, the figure is never fixed. It is translated through pressure, rhythm, colour and the systems that shape how a body is perceived.

## Body in *Resonance.*
Colour, rhythm and repetition expand the body into a perceptual field. Figure, environment and graphic system become different states of the same sensitive presence.

- Colour
- Rhythm
- Repetition
- Perception

### Chromatic Body
Colour as skin, memory and inhabited space.

### Optical Pulse
Symmetry and repetition activate movement in perception.
