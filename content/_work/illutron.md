## Context & Challenge

illutron brings artists, makers and technical practitioners together around experimental work in Copenhagen. Its floating cultural space is both a place for making and a point of contact with a wider public. Renovation raises a practical question: how can a collective maintain participation when access to its space is limited? The challenge extends beyond announcing events. People need to understand what is happening, how they can take part and how their involvement supports the organisation. ILLUTRON ANCHORS, the independent support association, provides an organisational context for this work, connecting membership, cultural activity and the longer-term conditions that make shared experimentation possible.

## Approach & Contribution

As Vice Chair of ILLUTRON ANCHORS, I contribute to organisational development and work across communication, community activity and creative projects. My contribution includes digital communication, newsletters, membership initiatives and the documentation of artistic, technical and organisational processes. Working with artists, makers and technical practitioners, I connect public invitations with the activities they describe. The Meet & Build invitation and programme shown below make this contribution concrete: they explain opportunities for participation and introduce the community around the barge. These materials document communication outputs. They are presented alongside the project context so that the relationship between organisational work, shared making and public access remains visible.

Documentation pending: digital analysis charts, Meet & Build photographs and verified participation results.
