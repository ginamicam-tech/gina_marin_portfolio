## Context & Challenge

Morhaven began as a holistic retreat concept in Cornwall, with a brief rooted in nature, Celtic heritage and quiet transformation. Before individual communication pieces could be developed, the project needed a shared identity and a clear position. The challenge was to connect the retreat’s intended character with a visual language that could work across print and digital contexts. Circles, elemental symbols and coastal references offered a foundation for that language. The existing archive contains concept research, a brand manual, an icon system, editorial applications and website concepts. These materials document the development of the brand as a system rather than an isolated logo.

## Approach & Contribution

I developed the positioning and launch strategy alongside the identity, connecting audience considerations with decisions about narrative, logo, colour and typography. The work extended into visual elements, photography direction, editorial applications and digital concepts. A recurring logic of circles and elemental symbols helped connect these outputs while leaving space for different uses. The guidelines shown below make those decisions available as a practical reference, and the application studies demonstrate how the identity can operate across formats. The documented outcome is a brand foundation and communication direction for taking the retreat concept towards launch; the website studies are presented as concepts, not as evidence of a completed launch.
