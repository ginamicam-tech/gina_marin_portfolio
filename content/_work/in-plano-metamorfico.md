    ## About the work

    This print brings together eyes, faces, mechanical forms and lines that suggest waves or frequencies. The body appears in transition: recognisable, but also being transformed by the structures around and within it.
- layout: newspaper
  text: |-
    ## Making the work

    I worked with etching and aquatint on a metal plate, exploring how incisions and texture move from metal to ink and paper. The printing process became part of the enquiry: each material gives the image a different weight, resistance and surface.
